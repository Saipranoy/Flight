Emergency rules

How PDG Flights decides what to show a stuck traveller. Based on rules.md.

Always true

Tone

Warm throughout, short, times and actions first. No promises, no jokes.

Safety

Only set off if the road is safe. If officials say stay put, stay. Show the local emergency number. Never imply a seat is booked or held.

Never

Show a flight they can't reach. Mark a flight in time when it misses the deadline. Drop a need silently.

Look

PDG Light. Status always has a word. Stamps never cover times, airports or prices. Nothing essential below 12px.
